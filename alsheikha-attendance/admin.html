Add / Register New Employee & Documents

Basic Salary is fixed at 1,000 QAR for every employee — "Other Allowances" shown throughout the system is simply Total Salary minus Basic. Overtime is now calculated automatically per Qatar Labour Law (no manual OT rate needed). Rest days are EARNED at 1 day per every 6 days actually worked (not tied to a fixed weekday, applies identically in every month) — an employee with 0 worked days has earned 0 rest days, so every unworked day with no leave/manual deduction is an automatic absence deduction, and working more days than the earned-rest balance implies were needed pays a bonus for the extra day(s).

Employee Annual Leaves

Every employee is entitled to 21 days of annual leave per year, with a 750 QAR annual leave allowance and a 750 QAR annual air-ticket allowance — both paid ONCE per eligibility year, automatically added to the salary of the month the leave starts in. The days the employee is away are deducted at the normal daily rate for that same month, exactly like any other dated deduction.
